Flashcards

Q: Why are bees important to our food supply?

A: Bees pollinate about one-third of the crops humans eat, including fruits, nuts, and vegetables.

Q: Do all bees make honey?

A: No. Only honeybees make honey — most bee species are solitary and don’t produce any.

Q: How do bees find their way home?

A: Bees navigate using the sun, landmarks, and even the Earth’s magnetic field.

Q: What is the “waggle dance”?

A: It’s how bees communicate distance and direction to food sources through movement patterns.

Q: How many flowers can a bee visit in one day?

A: A single bee can visit 2,000+ flowers per day while collecting nectar and pollen.

Q: Can bees recognize human faces?

A: Yes! Studies show bees can remember and distinguish human faces using pattern recognition.
